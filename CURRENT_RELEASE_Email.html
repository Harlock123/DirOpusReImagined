DirOpus Reimagined v0.1.37.0
A fast, cross-platform dual-panel file manager — inspired by the classic Amiga Directory Opus.

Hi! I've been building a file manager and just released a new version — thought you might like to try it. It runs on Windows, macOS, and Linux (Intel & ARM), it's free and open source, and there's nothing to install — just unzip and run.

What it does
  • Two folders side by side — copy, move, and compare between them, with folder tabs on each side.
  • All the file basics — copy / move / rename / delete, drag-and-drop, copy and paste with other file managers, delete-to-Trash (recoverable), and undo for renames, moves and deletes.
  • Compare & sync two folders with a click, compare two files side by side, and find duplicates.
  • Cloud storage built in — Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Amazon S3, and 50+ more, plus your own SFTP, SMB, FTP and WebDAV servers, browsed like local folders.
  • Archives like folders — open .zip, .7z, .rar, and .tar, copy files into, rename and delete inside .zip and .tar archives, and save edits back into them — even archives inside archives, password-protected zips and 7z, .tar.zst and .tar.xz, and .iso disk images.
  • Batch rename — rename a whole selection with a live before→after preview, find&replace (regex), and numbering.
  • Make it yours — banks of 36 custom buttons to launch your own tools, plus bookmarks, a folder tree, a command line, scripts, and a built-in file viewer and hex editor.
  • Themes — Light/Dark, Dracula, Nord, Solarized and High Contrast — in English, German, French or Spanish.
  • Fast to drive — search by name or by what’s inside files, recent folders, flat view, a command palette, type-ahead jumping, and full keyboard control with your own shortcuts.
New in 0.1.37:
  • Scripts and remote control — tell DORI what to do from a button, a script, or another program (DORI --cmd “go ~/Pictures; select *.jpg; copy”), like the Amiga original’s ARexx port.
  • Previous versions — open or restore older copies of a file from Btrfs and ZFS snapshots, Time Machine or Windows shadow copies.
  • Find similar photos — spot resized copies, re-saved JPEGs and burst shots, keep the best and trash the rest.
  • Remove photo details — take out where a photo was taken (or every detail) before sharing it, without touching the picture.
  • PDF tools — merge PDFs, split them, take out pages or turn pages.
  • More archive types — make .tar.zst, .tar.xz and password-protected .7z archives, Extract Here, and Test Archive to find damaged files.
  • As administrator — delete, copy, move or rename protected files after the system asks for your password.
  • Cloud extras — copy a share link to a cloud file, or mount a cloud as a drive other programs can open.
  • Settings to go — export and import all your settings as one file, or keep them with the program on a USB stick.
  • Languages and accessibility — menus and dialogs in German, French and Spanish, screen-reader support, and a High Contrast theme.
  • Earlier, in 0.1.28 to 0.1.36: find and replace in files, a trash browser, a slideshow, a hex editor, Connect to Server (SFTP, SMB, FTP, WebDAV), drive eject, audio and video conversion, labels other apps can see, update checks, a folder tree, a command line, renaming by photo date and music tags, password-protected zips, .iso disk images, quick editing in the viewer, saved sync jobs, Go to Folder, drag and drop with other apps, copy and paste with other file managers, folder rules, disk catalogues, archives inside archives, a speed limit and Start Later, folder sizes, natural sort, video thumbnails, exporting listings, secure delete, disk usage, labels and tags, a git status column, image tools, synced browsing, copies that carry on, splitting and joining files, undo for deletes, renames and moves, comparing files, flat view, finding duplicates, the command palette, saved layouts and filters, your own shortcuts, photo details, search inside files, banks of buttons and checksums.
Fixed: since 0.1.19, copying or moving to and from cloud folders, and copying or moving files out of archives, didn’t work. 0.1.28 fixed that, and 0.1.29 fixes a stray line left around the active panel after resizing the window, and button labels cut off in small windows.
